# Design Context — SOLARIS LUX

## Users
### Primary Audience
Tech-savvy students (ISTA Témara) and tech enthusiasts who appreciate premium, high-performance gear. They value technical precision, speed, and a unique "Gothic Sanguine" aesthetic.

### Context
Browsing for high-end tech accessories, specifically the Solaris Lux powerbank, in a cinematic and immersive digital environment.

## Brand Personality
- **Dark**: Deep obsidian backgrounds with dramatic lighting.
- **Technical**: Precise stats, monospace details, and 3D visualization.
- **Sanguine**: Bold red accents (`#880808`) and gold glows (`#B8860B`).

## Aesthetic Direction
- **Primary Theme**: Dark Mode (Sanguine Edition).
- **Secondary Theme**: Light Mode (Sage Edition).
- **Tone**: Brutally minimal but cinematic. High contrast, sharp edges, and large typography.

## Design Principles
1. **Cinematic Hierarchy**: Use dramatic scale (huge type) and focal points (3D models) to guide the eye.
2. **Technical Precision**: Every label and value should feel intentional, using monospace fonts for data.
3. **Glass & Obsidian**: Use glassmorphism and deep black surfaces to create depth without clutter.
4. **Intentional Rhythm**: Alternate between tight technical groupings and generous whitespace.
